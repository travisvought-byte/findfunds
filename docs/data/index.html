Open data

Every listing is free to reuse under CC BY 4.0. Please credit "FindFundraisers.org, a service of Veteran Home Guardians".

  • events.json: every listing, with host, venue, dates, times, source and trust level.
  • events.csv: the same list as a spreadsheet, one row per listing.

Each event page also carries schema.org Event markup for its upcoming dates. Fields follow schema.org Event names where they exist; "trust" is "public_list" (from someone else's list, not yet confirmed), "organizer" (confirmed by the organizer) or "claimed" (kept up to date by the organizer).

FindFundraisers.org is a free community service of Veteran Home Guardians, an Ohio 501(c)(3). Listings are free. We never take money for the groups listed here: every gift goes straight to the group through its own link or at the door.
