Open data

Every list is free to reuse under CC BY 4.0. Please credit "Ohio Community Events, a service of Veteran Home Guardians".

  • series.json and series.csv: recurring events (fairs, festivals, fish fries, holiday events and more) with place, organizer, last and next dates, source and last-checked date.
  • organizers.json and organizers.csv: the groups that run events, with address, phone, website, events calendar, source and last-checked date.
  • events.json and events.csv: dated fish fry listings with flyers.

Event pages carry schema.org Event markup only for dates that are posted for the coming season. "trust" says how far a record was checked: "official_list" (from an official roster), "public_list" (from someone else's list), "organizer_page" (checked on the organizer's own site), "organizer" (confirmed by the organizer) or "claimed" (kept up to date by the organizer).

Ohio Community Events is a free community service of Veteran Home Guardians, an Ohio 501(c)(3). Listings are free. We never take money for the groups listed here: every gift goes straight to the group through its own link or at the door.
